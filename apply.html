Apply to Join Ironclad Tech

This form takes about three minutes. Have your CV ready as a PDF or Word file, and find a quiet spot — we ask for a short voice recording so we can hear your spoken English. Applications are only accepted here, not by WhatsApp or social media message.

Digits only. Base salary expectation, before commission.

3. Experience

Freshers are welcome. Strong English matters more to us than experience.

4. Your CV

PDF or Word. Maximum 5 MB. If you do not have a CV, write your details in a Word file and upload that.

5. Voice sample strongly recommended

This is the most important part of your application. Read the three lines below out loud, clearly and at a normal speed. It takes about forty-five seconds. Do not worry about sounding perfect — we only want to hear how you speak English.

“Hello, good afternoon. My name is ______ and I am calling on a recorded line from Ironclad Tech. I am reaching out today about the final expense benefit programme available to residents in your state. It only takes two minutes, and I would like to check whether you already have coverage in place. May I ask you a few quick questions?”
0:00

By submitting you agree to our Privacy Policy. We never share your information with third parties. Applications are reviewed in the order they arrive — please do not submit twice.

What happens after you submit

  1. You receive an email with a reference number, usually within a minute.
  2. Our recruitment team reviews your CV and listens to your voice sample.
  3. If you are shortlisted we call or email you to arrange an interview.
  4. You hear from us either way.

Problems with the form? Email careers@getironcladtech.com and tell us what went wrong. Please do not send CVs by WhatsApp or social media message — those are not monitored for applications and will not reach our recruitment team.

Now Hiring — Priority Role

Fronter / Cold Calling Agent

On-site, Haroon Chowk, Rawalpindi   Full-Time, Monday to Friday   7:30 PM – 4:00 AM   2 openings, female candidates

As a Fronter you are the first voice our United States customers hear. Working from a dialer and a proven script, you introduce our Final Expense insurance offering, gauge interest, and transfer qualified prospects to a licensed closer. You are not asked to close sales or handle payments — your job is to open conversations well, and often.

What you will be doing

  • Making outbound calls to US-based prospects from our dialer
  • Delivering the Final Expense opening pitch clearly and confidently
  • Qualifying interest against simple criteria and warm-transferring to a closer
  • Logging call outcomes accurately in our system
  • Working to a daily transfer target, with your team lead's support

What we are looking for

  • Clear, confident spoken English — this matters more to us than anything else on your CV
  • Comfortable spending a full shift on the phone, and able to handle rejection
  • Basic computer skills — web applications and typing
  • Able to commit to an on-site night shift, 7:30 PM – 4:00 AM, Monday to Friday
  • Previous outbound, Final Expense, ACA or Medicare experience is an advantage, but we hire and train freshers with strong English

What we offer

  • Competitive base salary plus commission on every successful transfer, discussed at interview
  • Saturday and Sunday off — a real five-day week, which is uncommon on a US calling floor
  • Paid training on the campaign, the script and the dialer
  • Clear promotion path: Fronter → Senior Fronter → Closer → Team Lead

Both of these openings are for female candidates. If shift timing or travel is the only thing holding you back, apply anyway and tell us in the form — we would rather talk it through than lose a good candidate.

How to apply: use the form below. It takes about three minutes, and it asks you to record a 45-second voice sample reading a sample opener — that is how we assess spoken English, so just be clear and natural. Applications are accepted through the form only; CVs sent by WhatsApp or social media message do not reach our recruitment team.

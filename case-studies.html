Healthcare
Illustrative Example

Cutting No-Shows for a Multi-Location Medical Practice

The Situation

A multi-location primary care group was struggling with missed appointments and an overwhelmed front desk fielding constant reschedule calls.

What We Did

Deployed a dedicated scheduling team running proactive reminder calls, confirmations and same-day rebooking workflows integrated with the practice's existing calendar system.

Typical Outcomes

Fewer No-Shows

via proactive reminders

Lighter Front-Desk Load

less time on reschedule calls

Fast Go-Live

typically live within weeks

Insurance
Illustrative Example

Scaling Lead Verification for an ACA Enrollment Agency

The Situation

An ACA-focused agency was losing licensed-agent hours to unqualified inbound leads during open enrollment.

What We Did

Built a verification team that screens every lead against eligibility criteria before transferring it to a licensed agent.

Typical Outcomes

Higher-Quality Transfers

fewer unqualified handoffs

More Leads Verified

per agent, per day

Agent Time Recovered

for closing, not screening

AI Voice
Illustrative Example

24/7 AI Reception for a Service Business

The Situation

A service business was missing after-hours calls and losing bookings to competitors who answered first.

What We Did

Deployed an AI voice agent handling after-hours answering, qualification and direct calendar booking, with live handoff to a human agent during business hours.

Typical Outcomes

More Calls Answered

including after-hours

More Bookings Captured

automatically, overnight

Instant Answer Time

no hold, no voicemail
